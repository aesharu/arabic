Najdi scriptنجدي

28 letters in 12 days, learned by shape rather than alphabet order. Two days per group.

Tap a big letter to hear its name, or a word to hear it said. Your phone's voice speaks standard Arabic, so for ق trust the Najdi note, not the voice.

Write every letter by hand, in every form, until you stop hesitating. Your hand learns shapes faster than your eyes do.
